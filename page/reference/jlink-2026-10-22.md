# J-Link throughput reference

The probe-rs results drive a J-Link Ultra through the same probe-rs revision, 4 KiB workload,
MSPM0L1306 and 10-pin cable as the MCU-Link measurements, so they compare the probes under one
host. They were taken on 2026-10-22, two days before the MCU-Link set. The toggle on the main
charts overlays them at the clocks both sets share. Each rate is 4,000,000 divided by the median
4096-byte transfer time in microseconds, which gives KiB/s. Two runs completed at 1, 2, 5, 10, 20
and 30 MHz. Both timed 50 MHz attempts failed with SWD protocol errors, so the overlay has no
50 MHz point.

The J-Link section graphs JLinkExe's `TestRSpeed` and `TestWSpeed` results in SEGGER's KByte/s,
where a KByte is 1024 bytes. They come from SEGGER's own host and a different workload, so the page
keeps them apart from the probe-rs charts. JLinkExe times its whole 8 × 4 KB test in whole
milliseconds, so its figures from 20 MHz up carry 2 to 5% of rounding.

All measurements were taken on an erased and halted MSPM0L1306, with SEGGER's software V9.88 and
the probe's firmware of 2022-09-22.
