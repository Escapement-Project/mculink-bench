# J-Link throughput reference

The page shows the 4 KiB probe-rs results as a separate probe and target reference. Rates are
calculated from the median 4096-byte transfer times in `probe-rs-summary.txt`: 4,000,000 divided by
the median time in microseconds gives bytes per second, then divided by 1024 gives KiB/s. Two
usable runs completed at 1, 2, 5, 10, 20 and 30 MHz. Both timed 50 MHz attempts failed with SWD
protocol errors, so the chart has no point there.

The second table reports JLinkExe `TestRSpeed` and `TestWSpeed` output in its original KByte/s
units. It uses a separate SEGGER DLL measurement path. Do not compare those figures directly with
the probe-rs chart.

The target was an MSPM0L1306. SEGGER attach erased its main flash, which was not restored. The
target was left halted. See the local project record at
`RESULTS.md`, which is not published, for the full setup, raw files and limitations. The J-Link
figures are a distinct reference, not MCU-Link or stock firmware results.
