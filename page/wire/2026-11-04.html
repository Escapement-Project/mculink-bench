MSPM0L1306analyser at 1 ns on SWCLK and SWDIOrecorded 2026-11-04

These are recordings of the wire itself, every clock edge of a 64 KiB flash read at each clock asked. Hover over or tap an underlined figure to see the stretch of wire it comes from. The analyser's leads load the lines: with them on, the probe samples read data a cycle later at 50 and 37.5 MHz than it does without them.

Asked for 50 MHz, Verge sends each request at 50 MHz and writes data at 50 MHz. It reads data at 37.5 MHz. Before the first bit of read data, the probe checks the acknowledge. That bit's period stretches to about 80 ns.

Capturing energy at 100,000 pairs a second as well, Verge reads data at 30 MHz when asked for 50 or 37.5. Its writes keep their clock, but 14 of 95 writes at 50 MHz have one data period 8 ns longer.

NXP's firmware, asked for 10 MHz, reads data at 6.9 MHz and writes at 7.1. Asked for 25 or 50 MHz, it reads at 10.0 MHz and writes at 10.7. Its read clock is uneven: 93 and 100 ns periods take turns in every read. At 10 MHz, 13 of 106 writes stop for about 6 µs in the middle of the word. Asked for 20 MHz with the leads on, it did not connect.

These are recordings of the wire itself, every clock edge of probe-rs's runs. Hover over or tap an underlined figure to see the stretch of wire it comes from.

Asked for 30 or 50 MHz, Verge's TCK runs at 30 MHz in its fast bursts: 85% of their periods are 33 ns. Most of the rest are 53 ns, where the generator takes a longer step. Capturing energy at 100,000 pairs a second as well, it keeps 83% of them at 33 ns.

NXP's firmware runs TCK in bursts at two rates 14 ns apart, about half each. Asked for 10 MHz, its bursts run at 173 ns and 187 ns, 5.8 and 5.3 MHz. Asked for 20, 30 or 50 MHz to read 256 KiB, it drives the same wire each time, and the read fails.

In cJTAG every JTAG bit is a frame of packets on TMSC. In OScan1 a frame has three: inverted TDI, TMS and TDO. With packets at 50 MHz, Verge's frames take 127 ns or 133 ns, a JTAG clock of 7.9 or 7.5 MHz. The TDO packet is the long one: the probe waits out the target's TDO delay before ending it.
