# Throughput, 2026-10-21: CPU 0 stages commands

Build `a93b60c` moved two copies from CPU 1 to CPU 0. CPU 0 copies each command out of USB memory
as it arrives, and copies each reply back as it sends it. Vendor command `0x8C` turns this off at
runtime, so one image was measured both ways, with nothing else changed. Two images would also
have differed in flash layout.

The matrix is `swd-1`, on the standalone MCU-Link and the L1306, as on 2026-10-20. Staging
alternated on and off, two runs each way. Results are in
`results/throughput/2026-10-21-gcc-stage-{on,off}-{1,2}.json`. Every case completed in all four
runs, probe-rs's included.

With 8 commands queued, the change with staging on, from the mean of each way's two runs:

| command size | below 50 MHz | at 50 MHz | each way's two runs within |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| a full packet: 101-word DAP_Transfer writes, 126- and 127-word blocks | 0.0 to +0.9% | +1.2 to +2.0% | 0.2% |
| 32 words | 0.0 to +1.2% | +1.8 to +2.4% | 0.3% |
| 8 words | +0.1 to +2.6% | -0.1 to +5.5% | 15.6% |
| 1 word | -11 to +29% | -24 to +31% | 15.1% |

- Commands of 32 words and more gain up to 2.4% at 50 MHz. Their runs repeat within 0.3%.
- 8-word commands at 50 MHz vary by up to 15.6% between runs.
- 1-word commands move both ways, in 23 of 40 cases by more than their runs vary. Staging saves
  CPU 1 only a few bytes of copying for them. This run does not show why they move.
- With 1 command queued, commands longer than a word change by -4.5% to +4.0%, median +0.1%.
- With 2 queued, 108 of those 120 cases gain, by a median of 0.4% and at most 2.2%.
- probe-rs's own reads and writes do not move: the median change is +0.1%, against 1.5% between
  runs.
