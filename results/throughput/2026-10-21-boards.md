# Throughput and the wire, 2026-10-21: the Pro against the plain MCU-Link

One image, build `49633f3` with GCC, measured on both boards. First on an MCU-Link Pro, with the
L1306 on its debug connector J7, then on the plain MCU-Link. Each board ran both matrices,
`swd-1` and `wire-1`. Results are in `results/throughput/2026-10-21-{pro,plain}-gcc.json` and
`results/wireclock/2026-10-21-{pro,plain}-gcc.json`. The analyser was at the L1306's header with
its ground connected, as in `results/wireclock/2026-10-21.md`.

## Throughput

All 480 cases completed on both boards, probe-rs's included. 458 of them are within 3% between the
boards. Of the other 22, 16 are 1-word commands with 8 queued. The other six:

| case | the Pro against the plain board |
| --- | --- |
| 1-word block write, 5 MHz, 1 queued | +26.9% |
| 32-word DAP_Transfer DP read, 50 MHz, 1 queued | +6.9% |
| 8-word block DP read, 50 MHz, 8 queued | +5.1% |
| 32-word block read, 20 MHz, 1 queued | -4.5% |
| 8-word block read, 1 MHz, 1 queued | -3.3% |
| 1-word DAP_Transfer write, 1 MHz, 1 queued | -3.3% |

The 26.9% is the plain board's low figure. Its three runs gave 42 to 49 KiB/s, against 55 on the
Pro, and against 55 and 56 for the same case at 10 MHz.

## The wire

No command failed at any clock on either board. Data-bit periods agree between the boards within
0.8 ns at every clock. The analyser resolves both boards' SWCLK pulses from 1 to 30 MHz, and
neither's at 40 and 50 MHz, as `results/wireclock/2026-10-21.md` explains.
