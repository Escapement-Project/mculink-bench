# Matched throughput retake, 2026-10-24

The three result files use the same `swd-1` matrix, bench commit `d0bbc37`, probe-rs revision
`7b17871e1d60f68881a28841bcf1c8763345ea50`, host and target:

- `2026-10-24-gcc.json`: mculink-verge `e7c7acf`, GCC, 480/480 CMSIS-DAP cases passed.
- `2026-10-24-clang.json`: mculink-verge `e7c7acf`, Clang, 480/480 cases passed.
- `2026-10-24-stock.json`: NXP V3.172, 288 cases measured. At 20 and 50 MHz, all 96 cases per
  clock failed during attach because the DPIDR read returned count 0, ACK 3. This records the
  observed attach failure; it does not establish the reason. The probe-rs run returned success and
  reported data through 10 MHz only.

The plain MCU-Link `CJ6B19D4E7` was connected to the MSPM0L1306 (DPIDR `0x6ba02477`). The harness
halts the target, saves and checks its SRAM test page, then restores it. It requested 1, 5, 10, 20
and 50 MHz, with queue depths 1, 2 and 8, three repeats and 250 ms per run. The JSON records
reported clocks, host details, command results and image hashes.

The target's main flash had been erased during the J-Link attach on 2026-10-22. No image was
restored before this retake. The target stayed halted; the harness changed only the SRAM test page.

At 10 MHz and depth 8, full-packet AP block reads reached 819 KiB/s with GCC and 820 KiB/s with
Clang, against 536 KiB/s with stock. AP block writes reached 826 and 827 KiB/s, against 555 KiB/s
with stock. For probe-rs 4 KiB transfers at 10 MHz, GCC read 678 KiB/s and wrote 738 KiB/s; stock
read 492 KiB/s and wrote 521 KiB/s. Stock has no comparable 20 or 50 MHz result in this retake.

The exact build inputs and checksums are archived locally in
a directory outside this repository. The stock image stays out of it too. The
results retain its SHA-256 and the image's reported V3.172 identity.
