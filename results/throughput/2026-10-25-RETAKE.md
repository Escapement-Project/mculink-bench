# Matched throughput retake, 2026-10-25

The three result files share the `swd-1` matrix, bench commit `e1f0920`, probe-rs revision
`7b17871e1d60f68881a28841bcf1c8763345ea50`, the host and the target. They replace the 2026-10-24
set, whose stock run was V3.172 rather than the current release.

- `2026-10-25-gcc.json`: mculink-verge `2edfec6`, GCC, 480 of 480 cases passed.
- `2026-10-25-clang.json`: mculink-verge `2edfec6`, Clang, 480 of 480 cases passed.
- `2026-10-25-stock.json`: NXP's firmware V3.174 (called stock below), as LinkServer 26.9.130 ships
  it. Its `DAP_Info` reply also reports 3.174. 294 cases passed. All 96 cases failed at 20 MHz, and
  90 of 96 at 50 MHz. Nearly every failure is an acknowledge read as 3, which is not a valid SWD
  acknowledge, partway through a command. probe-rs measured stock through 10 MHz. It could not
  attach at 20 MHz, and the target stopped answering it at 30 and 50 MHz.

The standalone MCU-Link `CJ6B19D4E7` was connected to the MSPM0L1306 (DPIDR `0x6ba02477`). The
harness halts the target, saves and checks its SRAM test page, and restores the page afterwards. It
asked for 1, 5, 10, 20 and 50 MHz, at queue depths 1, 2 and 8, with three repeats of 250 ms each.
The JSON records the clocks the probe reported, the host, every command result and each image's
SHA-256. The stock image stays out of this repository.

The target's main flash has been erased since 2026-10-22, and the target stayed halted. The
harness changed only the SRAM test page.

At 10 MHz and depth 8, full-packet AP block reads reached 819 KiB/s with both builds, against
536 KiB/s with stock. Block writes reached 826 KiB/s against 555 KiB/s. At 50 MHz, our builds read
2,700 KiB/s and wrote about 3,280 KiB/s; stock has no result there. probe-rs's 4 KiB transfers at
10 MHz read 701 KiB/s and wrote 727 KiB/s with GCC, 682 and 727 KiB/s with Clang, and 491 and
520 KiB/s with stock.

Read single probe-rs figures at the top clocks with care. Earlier the same night, the same two
images ran on the harness before `e1f0920`. probe-rs's fitted per-byte rate differed between the
two runs by up to 12% at 30 and 50 MHz, in both directions, and by 4% or less at 10 MHz.
