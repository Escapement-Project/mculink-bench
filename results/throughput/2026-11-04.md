# Throughput, 2026-11-04: ours on df11e14 against stock V3.174, over SWD, JTAG and cJTAG

`tools/throughput.py` on the MCU-Link Pro, one unit flashed in turn with each image. Ours is
mculink-verge `df11e14`, built with gcc and with clang, each taken twice (`r1`, `r2`). Stock is
NXP's V3.174, flashed by `verge update --stock`. The host is probe-rs 17beb8e80 for every probe.
SWD ran on an MSPM0L1306 LaunchPad (matrix `swd-1`: the custom host and probe-rs). JTAG and cJTAG
ran on a CC1312R1 LaunchPad (`jtag-1`, `cjtag-1`: probe-rs only). Nothing was attached to the
lines. Results in `results/throughput/2026-11-04-*.json`.

The figures below are the `r1` runs unless a cell says otherwise. Repeats agree within a few
percent, except probe-rs at 20 MHz and above, where the same image read up to 12% apart (as on
2026-10-25).

- Ours is faster through probe-rs at every clock stock completes: 5 to 7% at 1 MHz, 1.3 to 1.4
  times at 5 MHz, 1.4 to 1.5 at 10, 1.6 to 1.9 at 20 and 2.7 to 3.2 at 50 (all four of our runs against stock's
  `r1`).
- The swd-1 matrix measured 480 of 480 cases in each of our four runs. Stock measured 427 and 425:
  53 and 55 cases failed, all at 50 MHz with commands queued. probe-rs failed against stock at
  30 MHz in both runs, and at 50 MHz in one.
- probe-rs does not check what it reads. Stock read 64 KiB of flash right 10 times in 10 at 10 and
  20 MHz, 8 in 10 at 25 and 9 in 10 at 50. A comparison with stock should take its fastest clock
  with right data, 20 MHz, rather than its fastest figure. Against stock at 20 MHz, ours at 50 reads
  about 3.1 times as fast through probe-rs (2035 against 654 KiB/s).
- A single-word round trip through probe-rs takes 67 to 70 µs on every build from 10 MHz up. The
  host and USB set it, not the probe.
- Ours read 64 KiB of flash right 10 times in 10 at 50, 37.5, 30 and 10 MHz.

- `tools/dap.py` reads 127-word blocks in each cJTAG format, KiB/s after warm-up, gcc r1, gcc r2,

## Under energy capture

The Pro captured at 10,000 or 100,000 pairs a second while gcc r1's image ran the matrices.

| requested | JTAG, 10,000 | JTAG, 100,000 | OScan5, 100,000 | SWD, 100,000 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 10 MHz | 734 / 729 | 705 / 676 | 376 / 367 | 695 / 696 |
| 30 MHz | 1497 / 1388 | 1337 / 1348 | 597 / 616 | 1463 / 1546 |
| 50 MHz | 1494 / 1335 | 1337 / 1353 | 652 / 670 | 1559 / 2006 |

- At 10,000 pairs a second JTAG reads stay within the clean runs' spread, and writes at 30 and
  50 MHz come out 4 and 7% under the slowest clean run. At 100,000, 4 KiB JTAG reads and writes at
  30 and 50 MHz run at 1337 to 1353 KiB/s, under every clean run, and a single-word read
  takes 105 µs over JTAG against 81, and 145 µs in OScan5 against 103. SWD reads at 50 MHz lose 23%,
  since a read clock takes 5 cycles while capture runs (mculink-verge README.md, "Limits").
- Every read stayed right: 256 KiB 5 times in 5 over JTAG and cJTAG at 30 and 50 MHz at both rates,
  64 KiB of the L1306's flash 10 times in 10 at 50, 37.5 and 30 MHz, and 480 of 480 swd-1 cases.

## Stock's SWD wire against 2026-10-21

The same runs, recorded with the logic analyser's digital leads on the L1306's SWD lines. Stock
V3.174 on the Pro writes exactly as V3.172 did on the plain MCU-Link (`results/wireclock/2026-10-21.md`):
data bits at 10.72 MHz from 25 MHz requested, 7.14 at 10, 3.85 at 5, 1.85 at 2, 0.94 at 1. It reads
faster: 10.02 MHz against 9.60 from 25 MHz requested, 6.86 against 6.40 at 10, 3.76 against 3.56 at
5. The fastest period is still in the acknowledge, at 18.9 MHz. The firmware, the board and the
analyser all changed, so these runs do not say which moved the reads.
